Depa Vallarta

We also have an apartment in Nuevo Vallarta, and we like to share it when we are not using it. It sleeps two, with the ocean in front and a balcony looking over the pools and the palms.

Because it is ours and we use it, it is not always free: the first step is to check availability for your dates. It is rented separately and does not come with Villa Chimo.

The last boat to Chimo leaves in the afternoon, so almost nobody arrives the same day they land. That is what makes it work: you do not lose a day of your trip, coming or going.
