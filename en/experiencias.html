Experiences

Things here have a season. The whales arrive on December 8. The waterfalls run full when it rains. Octopus comes out year-round, before dawn.

Year-round

Kayaks

Paddle out from Chimo beach and follow the coast to coves with no trail to them. The water is flat in the morning; by midday the northwest wind picks up and it is time to come back.

Duration
By the hour or by the day
Run by
From the house

December 8 to March 23

Whale watching

Humpback whales come into Banderas Bay every winter to calve. The season dates are the ones published in Mexico's federal gazette, and operators need a permit and a certification course. We go out with someone who has both.

Duration
3 hours
Run by
Permitted operator

Year-round

Octopus fishing

With the people who do it every day. You leave before dawn and come back to eat what you caught. This is what Chimo lives on: octopus and fish are the village economy.

Duration
4 hours
Run by
Village fishermen

June to October, the rainy months

Cabo Corrientes waterfalls

In the rainy season the waterfalls in the sierra run full and the hills turn green. It is the best time to walk to them, and the cheapest time to come.

Duration
Half a day
Run by
Local guide

Year-round

Boat trip along the south shore

Yelapa, Quimixto, Majahuitas and the unnamed beaches in between. You stop where you want and eat wherever a kitchen is open.

Duration
Half a day
Run by
Chimo boatmen

Olive ridley May–February · green turtle October–March

Turtle release at Mayto

The camp has run since 2005 and protects four species. It takes visitors and volunteers, and you can take part in a release. It sits on the overland route, out through El Tuito.

Duration
Full day, by road
Run by
Campamento Tortuguero Mayto

Year-round

The raicilla route in El Tuito

El Tuito sits at 1,085 metres and runs five to ten degrees cooler than Vallarta. This is where the raicilla distilleries are, along with panela cheese, coffee and honey. Cabo Corrientes is an official stop on Jalisco's Raicilla Route.

Duration
Full day, by road
Run by
El Tuito distilleries

Year-round, best at dawn

Parrots and macaws

The jungle of Cabo Corrientes starts where the beach ends. You hear the macaws before you see them, and they pass in pairs at the same hour most days. The parrots move in flocks and make a racket.

Duration
2 hours
Run by
Local guide

21:00
