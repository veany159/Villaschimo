The village

Chimo

A fishing village in Cabo Corrientes, at the southern end of Banderas Bay. About 280 people live here and the catch is octopus. The beaches stay empty most of the year because getting here takes effort, and that is the point.

Experiences

What there is to do, and when you can do it

Year-round

Kayaks

Paddle out from Chimo beach and follow the coast to coves with no trail to them. The water is flat in the morning; by midday the northwest wind picks up and it is time to come back.

Duration
By the hour or by the day
Run by
From the house

December 8 to March 23

Whale watching

Humpback whales come into Banderas Bay every winter to calve. The season dates are the ones published in Mexico's federal gazette, and operators need a permit and a certification course. We go out with someone who has both.

Duration
3 hours
Run by
Permitted operator

Year-round

Octopus fishing

With the people who do it every day. You leave before dawn and come back to eat what you caught. This is what Chimo lives on: octopus and fish are the village economy.

4 hours
